## Reading it

- `check_symbols` finds almost every break but says `missing` too readily: all 138 of its false positives are names it
  reports "never seen" or seen only in earlier releases (none is a rejected keyword), and they cluster in five packages: pytest 42, sqlmodel 30, fastapi 13,
  langchain-community 11, weaviate-client 10 (then langgraph 9, django 6, matplotlib 6). The causes are in the table
  below. `diff_versions` rarely raises a false alarm because it only reports what changed, so a path it can't see gives
  no event; that same blind spot costs it recall when a member's class or module is what was removed.
- The pre-registered `diff_versions` rule counts every `param_removed` event on a keyword the call passes. Two of its
  seven false positives are litellm's `completion(...)` and `acompletion(...)`, whose named parameters 1.103 folded into
  `**kwargs`; DepTime reports them with `absorbed_by_var_keyword: true`, and a reader honouring that flag wouldn't count
  them (not pre-registered). One more is `StateGraph(State)`, where the rule counts a newly required parameter the call
  passes positionally. The other four are real false `removed` events (below).
- **Deprecations:** the pre-registered truth (a warning when the name is read, `__deprecated__`, or a deprecation note in
  the docstring) marks 84 working uses, and `check_symbols` flags 14 of them (16.7%). But most of those docstring notes
  are about a deprecated parameter (pandas' `read_csv` for `delim_whitespace`), not the name, so this measure says
  little. On warnings alone, 2 names qualify (3 uses: matplotlib's `rcsetup.interactive_bk`, weaviate's top-level
  `AuthApiKey`), and neither tool flags them (not pre-registered). The next set needs a better source of truth here
  (DT-QA-08).
